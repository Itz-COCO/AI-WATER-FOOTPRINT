AI Water Footprint Calculator
A web-based tool that estimates the hidden water cost of AI usage and helps users make more environmentally conscious choices.

The Problem
Training and running AI models requires massive amounts of water for data center cooling. A single ChatGPT conversation of 20-50 questions can use about 500 mL of water. Most users are unaware of this cost.

What This Does
This calculator lets users estimate how much water their daily AI usage consumes, making the invisible environmental cost visible at the point of use.

Features
- Per-provider water cost comparison (10 AI providers)
- Interactive bar chart showing water usage across providers
- Water saved calculator that incentivizes choosing smaller models
- Daily/weekly/monthly usage tracker with local data persistence
- Cheeky personality and motivational messages

Data Source
Water usage estimates are based on research from UC Riverside on the water footprint of large language models, including studies by Li et al. on ChatGPT water consumption.

How to Use
Visit the live site at: https://itz-coco.github.io/AI-WATER-FOOTPRINT/
Select an AI provider, enter your number of queries, and see your estimated water usage.
