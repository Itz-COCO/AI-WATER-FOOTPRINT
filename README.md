# AI Water Footprint Calculator
A tool to visualize the hidden water cost of AI usage.
## The Problem
Training and running AI models requires massive amounts of water for data center cooling. A single ChatGPT conversation can use up to 500ml of water. Most users are unaware of this cost.
## What This Does
This project provides a calculator that estimates how much water your daily AI usage consumes, making the invisible environmental cost visible at the point of use.
## Features
-Input your number of AI queries per day.
-See estimated water usage in milliliters.
-Based on research from UC Riverside.
## Why Built This
I wanted to create something that goes beyond awareness campaigns- a tool that actively helps users understand and reduce their AI water footprint.
